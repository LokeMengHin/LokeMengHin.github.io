Yoasobi


YOASOBI is a Japanese music duo formed in 2019, consisting of composer and producer Ayase and vocalist Ikura, whose real name is Lilas Ikuta. The duo is known for turning stories, novels, and other literary works into music, earning them the nickname "the novel-to-music duo." They gained widespread recognition with their debut single, 'Yoru ni Kakeru' (Into the Night), which became a major hit in Japan.

YOASOBI continued to achieve international success with popular songs such as 'Idol', the opening theme of the anime 'Oshi no Ko', and 'The Blessing', featured in 'Mobile Suit Gundam: The Witch from Mercury'. Their unique blend of J-pop, electronic sounds, and storytelling has attracted fans worldwide, establishing them as one of Japan's most influential modern music acts.
